Detailed Description

bootstrap equations in block form

The differentiated equations are

\[ \sum_b A_{ab} \, dz_b = dq_a \]

where \( A_{ab} \) is the sensitivity of curve \( a \)'s helper quotes to curve \( b \)'s nodes. Only interacting off-diagonal blocks are stored.
